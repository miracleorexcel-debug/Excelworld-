Love & Fate

Meeting you was fate from a thousand lifetimes.
Not luck, not chance—
my soul had been searching
long before I knew your name.
Every life before this one
was just practice.
Every heartbreak, every wrong turn,
was leading me here.
To you.

only hoping it leaves me with a sweet scent.

Life Is Like a Cube

Life is like a cube,
viewed from different angles.
The sages said,
“Live your life like there’s no tomorrow.”
But what if tomorrow comes and I’m unprepared?
Another time they said,
“Make plans for tomorrow.”
But what if tomorrow never comes?
Why care about what they say?
Live your life without regret.

Armored Steel

My spirit, built like armored steel,
my courage to follow my heart,
the resilience I once held—
gone like a deceased soul.
Now darkness dwells within me,
and my bright moon has dimmed.
When will my resilient spirit return?
Return, and let me conquer again.
When the darkness is finally gone
and the moon brightens up the night,
I shall raise a toast to myself.

My Thoughts

My thoughts ring in my head.
The sounds follow a trench’s path,
narrowing the words in my ears.
My heart creates the wording,
typing them like a typewriter.
My thoughts follow my heart, and my heart holds two options, always a yes or a no.
I made my choices and reaped the seeds.
If I followed my heart, which one would I choose,
a yes or a no?
My own thoughts are the world I created in my own mind.

The Pause of Time

Life has left me in a pause, with thoughts that have no answers.
Questions that my heart beats at and that leave my brain dormant.
Questions of how come and what ends.
Questions of how far and how long.
Questions that lack the piece of knowledge the world holds.
If I had never existed, what would have been my fate?
Some will advise me, but their advice always lacks the true piece.
Even the pause of time has no answers.

A Fairy From the Mountain

Her beauty is like a fairy descending from the mountain.
Every step makes my heart beat a thousand times.
Her dress is like dancing flowers.
And a little smile drove my soul into a dream.
Her eyes are like paradise,
telling more about her innocence.
Covered in rags, yet she gave me a hand
so I could stand.
But none could compare to her pure heart.
Even if it cost me a fortune,
I must dine with her today.

The Clearer Side

I have sided with my darkest side,
so I forgot the clearer side of life.
I had filled my mind with trash,
so I forgot the clearer side of my mind.
My hands and legs have touched dirty things,
so I forgot the clean things.
When my mind forgets, the whole body follows suit.
I stopped thinking as I did
and started thinking the opposite.
Now my mind is clearer than the white clouds in the sky.

A Day Spent

A day spent
can never return.
A day wasted is dust,
losing all its forms.
The sun comes and goes,
leaving nothing behind to prove
it was ever here.
Was it wine? Or women?
That turned the hours to ash?
A day spent lavishly
is still just dust,
because I squandered it
without ever knowing when.

Walking Through Life

I walk through life like walking through the day,
with my way darkened swiftly by a strong wind.
The leaves lift their butts from the ground
in the form of a torque,
the feeling leaving me
with the comparison of me walking through life.
