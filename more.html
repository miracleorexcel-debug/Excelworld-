A Seed That Refuses to Die

The road is full of thorns,
and I must still walk.
I might pause
when the sun moves to the west,
but never take a step backward.
I might cry when it rains,
but never for sorrow.
My cries water my seeds,
and the sun makes them green.
My heart sees what the eye can’t.
Even a thousand thorns
bow to a single seed
that refuses to die.

Playing It Safe

I was so scared to choose,
so I chose nothing—
and it cost me nothing.
But nothing ever grows.
Nothing ever moves.
So I chose.
And I failed, and
I grew too.
And the person I am now
could never have been born
from playing it safe.

Like the Moon

When the night was dark,
alone in my hut,
while others were partying and celebrating,
I looked at the lonely moon
and a thousand stars.
Yet only one moon alone
shone brighter in the sky
than a thousand stars.
That night I looked at the moon
and said to myself:
“May I stand out like the moon.
May my focus be set on the sky, never on the ground.”

The White Sparrow

The white sparrow flies in the sky
because he believes it’s possible.
I believe it is possible for me too,
but the white sparrow doesn’t just believe.
He works towards it, learning it day by day.
What use is belief without work?

The Clear Blue Sky

When I am sad,
I just look up.
I look at the blue sky.
I look deep into the white clouds.
I paint that picture in my mind.
It is so beautiful,
and the sadness goes away.

How many chances have I missed?

whether I’m ready or not.

The Season

Love is like a season.
It comes and goes.
I kept waiting for the season to return.
It came again—and went.
It became a cycle.
That left me broken.
The moment I realized true love exists within me,
the seasons never dared come again.

Love

When did love become a word
that we treat like sentences?
Always looking for the perfect words to replace the feelings.
Where do we keep the action while imitating the feelings
and modifying the sentences?
In our mouths, love and words have intertwined,
losing the very value it holds.
Even I can no longer distinguish between feelings and emotions.
They might say you are their lover while offering you poison.
Love is action and not a perfect sentence.

The Imperial Kitchen

Her love was like a catapult;
it hurled me down to earth.
The impact struck my forehead,
spilling my dishes to the underworld.
I learned that even a salty taste
cripples faster than a scorpion.
Yet when that love—
disguised as hate—tasted salty,
still, in my imperial kitchen,
only her love cooked the finest dish.
Was it for love,
or for the imperial kitchen,
that she chose me?
The underworld cried for me,
for even wealth is a curse
without love.

The Superhero

I heard the world needed the strongest,
so I started building up my muscle.
Then I heard the world needed the most powerful,
so I tried to be like a superhero.
Then I heard of the strongest and most powerful people on earth,
and none was like the superhero I tried to be.
Then I began wondering what being the most powerful meant,
only to realize it was the people with the most powerful minds
and no muscle.
I don’t want to be a superhero anymore.

The Nose

I forgot my nose’s existence,
the tiny but most important thing I have.
But when my nose stopped breathing
and the air was meaningless,
then I knew how important the nose was.
Seeing life coming out of me,
tears like water from my eyes,
the glimpse of the sun,
and nature’s voice.
Oh life, how fragile you are.
Even the nose is worth a fortune.

The Journey

Just starting the journey.
But which day will I look back and smile at who I was?
The journey is not hard—it’s simple.
But the choices life offers are harder to make.
I fear the responsibility of my wrong choices,
so I stepped back and never chose.
Yet I’m still stuck at the same level.
Later I asked myself, “Why didn’t I choose?”
The difference between who I am
and who I need to be
lies in these choices.
One wrong choice and you’re out of the game.
Not everyone makes a comeback.
If I fail, nobody will listen to my story.
So I muscle on—never to fail—
so the world will listen.

What Is This World

What really is in this world,
if not just to eat and enjoy?
What are we trying to achieve,
when we will all die and be forgotten in a century?
Where do we go after we die?
What if all of this is just a dream?
What if there is no rebirth?
What if there is no promise after death?
What happens to the things you never tried?
The places you never explored?
I sat and asked myself,
“What would my regrets be?”
The answers were simple:
the places I never went,
the things I never tried.

Worth It

What value does life hold
if I never do what I love?
What happiness can I claim
if I keep living in my past?
They were never my failures,
only the lessons I needed most.
They say, “Everything happens for a reason.”
But what difference does that make
to the one who carried the pain?
You never knew how deeply I hurt.
Then again,
everyone carries a burden
the world cannot see.
So why did mine
always feel the heaviest?
Yet my heart was greater
than the weight I carried.
I laid my pain to rest,
walked away,
and never looked back.

Rise and Fall of the Moon

Fading away like the moon,
yet up in the sky.
Not a single soul can tell
what is inside the moon.
The moon comes and goes.
Why does this pattern of the moon
resemble my rise and fall?
I cry and moan
when the winds come,
and when my moon dims,
I regret my repeated rise and fall.
But since ancient times,
who hasn’t followed the rise and fall of the moon?

Day and Night

Under the full moon, the world is painted black.
The owl and bat echoing,
the sound of crying babies,
and hearts carrying the weight of the world.
A lonely star still shines,
while beings hide in their shadows.
Slowly, darkness dissipates
before the dawn arises.
Birds fly by,
and the blue sky comes to life,
with a gentle morning sun
showing the beauty buried in both day and night.

The Lunar Eclipse

The lunar eclipse decides my shape,
splitting me into body and shadow.
The world takes sides.
My shadow is painted blank.
Then the moon comes back,
and under that brilliant light
my shadow ascends.

Where the Wind Dangles

The train slipped away.
My worn shoe surrendered,
unable to carry my weight.
So I stood still,
watching the day pass
with one foot bare.
Above the empty tracks,
the wind dangles.
Rise beyond the shadows.
A snail leaves its silver track,
and the flower bursts awake,
burying the forgotten past.

Dumb

I am dumb.
But being with the dumb
only makes me dumber.
I met the smart,
yet they think I am not enough.
Still dumb.
So I chose to learn from the smart
and use their knowledge
to build the world.

I tried it once—I said it was a mistake.

But I changed the day I looked my younger self in the eye.
