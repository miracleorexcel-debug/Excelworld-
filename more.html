The Clear Blue Sky

Looking up to the sky,
seeing the clear blue sky,
hoping it could ease my heart,
my heart that is filled with sorrow.
The sky is blue,
my heart is blue.
Yet the sky is calm,
and my heart is struggling to stay calm.

The night is my shield

A Fairy From the Mountain

Her beauty is like a fairy descending from the mountain.
Every step makes my heart beat a thousand times.
Her dress is like dancing flowers.

Even if it cost me a fortune,

The Journey

Just starting the journey.
But which day will I look back and smile at who I was?
The journey is not hard—it’s simple.
But the choices life offers are harder to make.
I fear the responsibility of my wrong choices,
so I stepped back and never chose.
Yet I’m still stuck at the same level.
Later I asked myself, “Why didn’t I choose?”
The difference between who I am
and who I need to be
lies in these choices.
One wrong choice and you’re out of the game.
Not everyone makes a comeback.
If I fail, nobody will listen to my story.
So I muscle on—never to fail—
so the world will listen.

What Is This World

What really is in this world,
if not just to eat and enjoy?
What are we trying to achieve,
when we will all die and be forgotten in a century?
Where do we go after we die?
What if all of this is just a dream?
What if there is no rebirth?
What if there is no promise after death?
What happens to the things you never tried?
The places you never explored?
I sat and asked myself,
“What would my regrets be?”
The answers were simple:
the places I never went,
the things I never tried.

Worth It

What value does life hold
if I never do what I love?
What happiness can I claim
if I keep living in my past?
They were never my failures,
only the lessons I needed most.
They say, “Everything happens for a reason.”
But what difference does that make
to the one who carried the pain?
You never knew how deeply I hurt.
Then again,
everyone carries a burden
the world cannot see.
So why did mine
always feel the heaviest?
Yet my heart was greater
than the weight I carried.
I laid my pain to rest,
walked away,
and never looked back.

Life Is Like a Cube

Life is like a cube,
viewed from different angles.
The sages said,
“Live your life like there’s no tomorrow.”
But what if tomorrow comes and I’m unprepared?
Another time they said,
“Make plans for tomorrow.”
But what if tomorrow never comes?
Why care about what they say?
Live your life without regret.

Rise and Fall of the Moon

Fading away like the moon,
yet up in the sky.
Not a single soul can tell
what is inside the moon.
The moon comes and goes.
Why does this pattern of the moon
resemble my rise and fall?
I cry and moan
when the winds come,
and when my moon dims,
I regret my repeated rise and fall.
But since ancient times,
who hasn’t followed the rise and fall of the moon?

The Superhero

I heard the world needed the strongest,
so I started building up my muscle.
Then I heard the world needed the most powerful,
so I tried to be like a superhero.
Then I heard of the strongest and most powerful people on earth,
and none was like the superhero I tried to be.
Then I began wondering what being the most powerful meant,
only to realize it was the people with the most powerful minds
and no muscle.
I don’t want to be a superhero anymore.
