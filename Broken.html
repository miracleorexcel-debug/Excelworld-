Broken

Home Love Broken Regret Betrayal

  1. I wake up and put on my clothes every morning.
    But did you forget something? The very person behind the clothes.
    It looks handsome and beautiful on me.
    But why do you always forget the one who wears them?
    You remember the clothes — but never me.
    Behind these clothes lies a hungry stomach.
    A person begging for help.
    Just needing a dollar.
    I may never tell you, because I have been rejected by 10 out of every 10 people I have met.
    Just a little to save me.
    No one knows my destiny.
    I might be the very person your children and grandchildren will wish they had known.
    But how many billionaires today once stood where I stand — needing just a dollar?

  2. A little rain came.
    The wind blew.
    And my little hut fell down.
    But don't mock me.
    I may have nothing now, but don't forget — many millionaires were once like me.
    They once lived in the worst of huts.
    Yet today they build skyscrapers.
    My hut fell, but my drive still remains.

  3. Ignorance in my mind.
    I followed the rules, the rules became my limits.
    How do I make it big if I won't break
    the shackles of my own limitations?
